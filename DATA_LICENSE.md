The MIT software license does not grant new rights to the original source images,
annotations, third-party metadata, or model initialization weights. Each source
retains its original terms. [The source catalogue](docs/sources.md) records known
dataset license declarations and links to the corresponding providers; an
`unknown` entry is not a grant of permission.

The manifest describes the authors' benchmark selection, label mapping, paths,
and partitions. Use it with lawfully obtained source datasets and retain the
attribution and usage conditions required by each provider. A dataset provider's
image license can differ from the associated article's or code repository's
license. OpenPlant does not apply one blanket image license to the collection.

The existing `model/*.pth` files are the published OpenPlant fine-tuned checkpoints.
Their use also depends on the applicable model/pretraining terms. The software
license should not be interpreted as relicensing third-party pretrained weights.

Figures in `assets/figures/` are reproduced from Liu et al., *Plants* 2026, 15(5),
727, DOI [10.3390/plants15050727](https://doi.org/10.3390/plants15050727), using the
authors' supplied paper assets. Cite the paper when reusing these figures and
observe any source-specific credits or third-party rights in the article. The
figure gallery is not a separate license to redistribute its underlying dataset
photographs individually.
